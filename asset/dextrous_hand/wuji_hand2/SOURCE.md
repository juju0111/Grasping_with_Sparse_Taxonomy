#### Wuji Hand 2 source

This standalone right-hand asset is adapted from Wuji Technology's
[`wuji-description`](https://github.com/wuji-technology/wuji-description) repository,
release **v2026.7.23** (commit `1407bee`, "Release v2026.7.23 (#18)"), specifically
`hand2/hand2_beta1/body/mjcf/right.xml` (md5 `9645050b96978b0f2639cad2c9363395`) and the
right-hand STL meshes under `hand2/hand2_beta1/body/meshes/right/`. Wuji Hand 2 (Beta 1)
is the first upstream revision with the coordinate conventions frozen (see the upstream
`CHANGELOG.md`, entry 2026.7.23). The upstream project is distributed under the MIT License
(Copyright (c) 2025 Wuji Technology); the license text is preserved verbatim in `LICENSE`.
Upstream documentation: <https://docs.wuji.tech>; vendor contact: support@wuji.tech.

The upstream 20 revolute joints, actuators, inertial parameters, convex mesh
collisions, and ten excluded assembly-overlap pairs are unchanged. RIM v6 adds
one root free joint, rotates the source `-Z` finger convention into the common
standalone-hand `+Z` convention, aligns the thumb-to-pinky spread with the
common `+Y` to `-Y` ordering, and provides the shared `hand_right`,
`box_center`, `box_top`, `wrist`, and `tip_0` through `tip_4` names. The common
`0.08 × 0.08 × 0.12 m` arm box is attached below the wrist. The original
anatomical fingertip sites remain available alongside the shared aliases.
The upstream model-level solver options are omitted so the composed scene owns
the MuJoCo integration, Jacobian, iteration, and tolerance policy without
attachment conflicts.

The five upstream `*_tip.STL` soft-pad meshes are retained for provenance and
future contact work, but remain unattached, matching the Hand 2 Beta 1 MJCF.
The upstream notes that its position-drive gains await Hand 2 system
identification.

#### Grit-format variants (generated)

`wuji_hand2_rh_w_fore_arm_touch_contact.xml` / `wuji_hand2_lh_w_fore_arm_touch_contact.xml`
are generated by `scripts/asset_tools/build_wuji_hand2_xml.py` from the official
`wuji-description/hand2/hand2_beta1/body/mjcf/{right,left}.xml` (left meshes `l_*.STL`
copied into `mesh/`). They add the shared Grit wrapper — `wuji_<rh|lh>:mocap` + welded
free palm `wuji_<rh|lh>_palm` at `0 0 1` (fingers +Z, palm +X, thumb +Y / −Y), forearm
box `wuji_<r|l>_arm_part`, per-link box touch sites + `<touch>` sensors, `self_coll`
contact sensors, fingertip bodies `wuji_<r|l>_<th|ff|mf|rf|lf>_end` / `_face_end` — and
rename joints/actuators to `wuji_<rh|lh>_*`, bodies to `wuji_<r|l>_*`. Upstream joints,
ranges, actuator gains, inertials and exclusions are unchanged.

The default `wuji_hand2_{rh,lh}_w_fore_arm_touch_contact.xml` (used by `config/wuji_hand2/grit_{right,left}.yaml`)
carries a Grit-tuned servo (the untouched official gains are kept in `*_official_gains.xml`): `kp=20 dampratio=1` on every finger actuator and default joint
`damping=0.05` (armature / ranges / forcerange unchanged). Chosen from the sweep in
`output/logs/left_hand/34_wuji_gain_sweep2.txt` to match the other hands' step response
(t90 ≈ 0.09 s, overshoot < 1 %, steady-state error 4e-4 rad at sim_dt 0.005); the official
gains gave 13 % overshoot and 0.025 rad error. Regenerate both variants with
`scripts/asset_tools/build_wuji_hand2_xml.py` (see `TUNED`).
